## Accuracy notes

- TAS uses the E6-B incompressible density-slide approximation; compressibility is not represented.
- True altitude assumes the ISA lapse rate between station and aircraft, as stated in the ASA manual.
- The high-speed wind slide is used for the B727 procedures. ASA p.28 confirms the 10-knot convention. The 100–1000 kt teaching range and normalized fivefold geometry are provisional: the local manual has no dimensioned accessory drawing. These are not asserted to be measured physical end marks.
- Off-course correction follows the 1-in-60 approximation and adds the parallel and closing corrections.
