Natural Earth 1:50m land, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/  (vector 5.1.2)

Cropped to the Australian region and stored as Resources/ownchart-coast.bin.
Rings wider or taller than 80° (Eurasia, Antarctica) are left out; Australia,
Tasmania, New Guinea, New Zealand and the nearby islands are kept.
Coordinates are signed hundredths of a degree, little-endian.
Packed by tools/pack-coast.py.

Resources/world-coast.bin contains all land rings from the same 1:50m source,
including Eurasia and Antarctica. It is packed with --global, with no regional
crop or duplicate closing points. Both native and web global maps use these
identical bytes. Source:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson
